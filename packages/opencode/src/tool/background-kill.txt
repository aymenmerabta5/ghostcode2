Stop a background job (task subagent or shell dev server) by its ID.

Params:
- task_id: ID of the job to kill (from background_list). Supports prefix matching on first 8 chars.
- reason: Optional reason for cancellation shown in confirmation.

Returns confirmation with final status. If job already completed/cancelled, notes that.
If job not found, fails with guidance to use background_list.

Killing a shell job terminates its child process (via scope close).
Killing a task subagent cancels its session and notifies parent.
